PageTurner Translation Sample: English to Korean

Mina carried a small e-ink reader in the side pocket of her travel bag. The screen looked plain in bright sunlight, but every page turned quickly and without glare.

At the station, she opened a short technical article about battery chemistry. She wanted the original English nearby, but she also wanted a Korean translation saved before the train entered the mountain tunnel.

The reader translated one paragraph at a time. It waited between requests, almost as if it were following her reading pace instead of rushing ahead.

When the signal disappeared, the saved translation still opened. Mina checked the cache status, smiled, and kept reading in offline mode.

Checklist:
- translate the current page
- prefetch the whole document
- reopen the saved translation
- compare English and Korean side by side
